IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono, regular (400) and semibold (600), are bundled
as unmodified WOFF2 files for offline use. They are served by the application;
the browser does not contact a font CDN.

Source: https://github.com/IBM/plex/tree/39276912b05f700ee1dd491950c35d8697214208

Files originate in `packages/plex-sans/fonts/complete/woff2/` and
`packages/plex-mono/fonts/complete/woff2/`. See `OFL.txt` for the upstream
SIL Open Font License and copyright notice.
